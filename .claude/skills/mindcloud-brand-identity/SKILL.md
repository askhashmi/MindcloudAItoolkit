# MindCloud (.sa) Brand Identity

Official brand identity extracted from the MindCloud Brand Identity Guidelines (mindcloud.sa).

## Brand Foundation

- **Core Goal:** Enabling entrepreneurship and innovation in Saudi Arabia.
- **Tagline:** Empowering Saudi Arabia's entrepreneurs and innovators.
- **Mission:** To provide Saudi founders with the scalable tools, support, and infrastructure needed to turn ideas into thriving ventures.

## Brand Colors

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| Primary 01 | Navy Dark | `#080E24` | 8, 14, 36 |
| Primary 02 | Navy Mid | `#0E2450` | 14, 36, 80 |
| Highlight | Bright Blue | `#1E6FC7` | 30, 111, 199 |
| Accent / Headings | Gold | `#F2A93C` | 242, 169, 60 |
| .sa Mark | Green | `#2E9E5B` | 64, 158, 91 |

Usage rules:
- Navy Dark (#080E24) is the dominant background color for dark layouts and the wordmark color on light backgrounds.
- Navy Mid (#0E2450) is the secondary dark surface (cards, alternate dark backgrounds).
- Bright Blue (#1E6FC7) is for highlights, links, and emphasis only — not large surfaces.
- Gold (#F2A93C) is reserved for accents and headings, used sparingly.
- Green (#2E9E5B) is the signature ".sa" mark color (the accent dot and ".sa" text). It also works for green key phrases in social posts (e.g., one highlighted word per headline).
- White/off-white is the standard content background for light layouts.
- Tints of each color (lighter steps) are allowed per the guideline's tint scale, but core brand colors take priority.

## Typography

- **English:** The Geometric Sans — all English communication. Weights available: Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold. Headings use Medium (500) and Bold (700).
- **Arabic:** Cairo — all Arabic content. Same weight range; headings in Medium (500) and Bold (700).
- Body text: Medium weight; Bold for emphasis.
- In web/HTML/PPTX work, use "Poppins" or a similar geometric sans as the English fallback, and Google Fonts "Cairo" for Arabic. Arabic layouts are always RTL.

## Logo

Structure: "Mind" in white inside a navy rounded rectangle + "Cloud" beside it + green accent dot + green ".sa" below-right.

Rules (from the guideline):
- Wordmark sits on a white rounded container (radius 6px) for contrast on dark navy backgrounds.
- Green accent dot signals the ".sa" regional mark — keep it small (~1/4 cap height).
- Minimum clear space = height of the "M" in "Mind" on all sides.
- On light backgrounds, drop the white container; keep the wordmark in navy (#080E24... guideline lists #0A0E27-family navy).

### Approved versions
- **Primary:** Navy wordmark on white / very light backgrounds.
- **High Contrast:** White logo version on dark backgrounds (Navy Dark or Navy Mid).
- **Colored:** Used selectively on high-contrast colored backgrounds (e.g., brand green).

### Prohibited (Logo Wrong)
- Do not distort proportions.
- Do not rotate or skew.
- Do not recolor any part of the logo.
- Do not place on unapproved colors or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Maintain clear space at all times. Use approved versions only.

## Social Media Specs

Profile pictures: Facebook 170×170 px, Instagram 320×320 px, LinkedIn 400×400 px — dark navy circle with white logo.

Covers/banners: Facebook 820×312 px, Instagram 1080×566 px, LinkedIn 1128×191 px — logo left, short 3-word message right (e.g., "Clarity. Focus. Growth."), green dot as a graphic accent.

Post style (SM Posts Guide):
- Square posts, dominant Navy Dark or clean white backgrounds; occasional full-green variant.
- Headline pattern: short punchy 2–4 word stacked lines ("Think. Connect. Grow." / "Clarity. Focus. Growth."), with one word or line highlighted in Green or Bright Blue.
- Small supporting line beneath the headline.
- Logo top-left; green dot as recurring graphic element (solid circle or dotted-circle path).
- Imagery: clouds, sky, network/particle meshes, minimal workspaces.

## Imagery Style

- Clouds and sky photography (aspirational, light).
- Digital network / particle mesh visuals in navy + green.
- Clean, bright, minimal workspaces; natural collaboration shots.
- The green sphere/dot as a physical motif (e.g., hand holding a green ball).
- City skylines above the clouds; Saudi urban context at night for dark covers.

## Application Rules (how to apply this skill)

1. Any MindCloud deliverable (PPTX, proposal, post, website, PDF) must use only the five brand colors plus white and their approved tints.
2. Dark layouts: Navy Dark background, white text, green/blue accents. Light layouts: white background, navy text, green accents.
3. Arabic content: Cairo font, RTL, conversational-professional Saudi tone.
4. Always include the green ".sa" dot motif somewhere as a brand signature when space allows.
5. PowerPoint decks follow the official template style: navy title slides with white/green Arabic titles, white content slides with navy headers and green icon accents, closing "شكرا لك" slide on navy.
6. Never mix this identity with other palettes (e.g., teal/orange AskHashmi palette) in the same asset — MindCloud assets use MindCloud colors only.
